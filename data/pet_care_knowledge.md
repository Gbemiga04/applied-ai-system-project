# PawPal Pet-Care Guidance

## Daily routines

Consistent daily routines help pets know what to expect. Feeding, walks, medication, and play should be adjusted for the pet's age, health, species, and veterinarian's instructions.

## High-priority tasks

Medication, urgent feeding instructions, water access, and veterinarian-directed tasks should usually receive the highest scheduling priority. Never change a prescription or medication dose without veterinary approval.

## Walking and exercise

Exercise needs vary by breed, size, age, health, and weather. A short walk may be appropriate for a senior or small pet, while a working dog may need a longer or more active outing. Avoid overexertion in extreme heat or cold.

## Feeding

Measure food portions according to the package and a veterinarian's guidance. Sudden diet changes can cause digestive problems. Keep fresh water available unless a veterinarian instructs otherwise.

## Medication

Medication times should match the prescription exactly. If a dose is missed, follow the medication label or veterinarian's advice rather than guessing or automatically doubling the dose.

## Schedule conflicts

Overlapping tasks should be separated when possible, especially if they involve exercise, medication, or a veterinary routine. Check task durations and move a lower-priority activity into an open time slot.

## Pet safety

Seek prompt veterinary guidance for breathing difficulty, collapse, poisoning, uncontrolled bleeding, repeated vomiting, seizures, or inability to pass urine. The advice in this knowledge base is general and does not replace an individual veterinary assessment.
